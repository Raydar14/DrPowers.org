Metta in the Wild: The Secret Kindness Game

Dr. Raychel Powers, Psy.D. October 17, 2026 Self-Kindness 2 min read

Vibe: Gentle, Mischievous, Grounded

What is Metta?

“Metta” is an ancient Pali word often translated as loving-kindness or friendliness. It isn’t about romantic love or even liking everyone you meet. It’s simply the wish for others to be well, happy, and free from suffering.

The rules of the game

The game is built on our favorite number: 3. You can play this anywhere: waiting for the train, sitting in a café, or walking to work.

Your mission: Identify 3 players in your vicinity and secretly send them a “bloom” of kindness.

Round 1: The Neutral Player

Look around. Find someone you don’t know: the barista, the person walking their dog, or someone scrolling on their phone across the room.

  • The move: Look at them (discreetly) and silently say: “May you be happy. May you be at ease.”
  • The goal: Realize that just like you, they have a story, a struggle, and a wish to be happy.

Round 2: The Challenge Player

This is the advanced level. Find someone who is slightly annoying you. Maybe it’s the person chewing loudly, the car cutting you off, or the slow walker in front of you.

  • The move: Instead of rolling your eyes, pause. Take one deep breath to anchor your energy. Silently say: “May you be safe. May you have peace.”
  • The goal: Feel your own nervous system settle. You aren’t doing this for them; you are doing it to release the tightness in your own chest.

Round 3: The Mirror Player

Pass a reflection in a shop window, a mirror, or just close your eyes and picture yourself.

  • The move: Offer the same wish inward. “May I be kind to myself today. May I be at ease.”
  • The goal: Remember that you deserve the same kindness you just gave away.

Why play?

Because it shifts the world without changing a thing.

When we play Metta in the Wild, we stop scanning for threats and start scanning for humanity. It turns a boring wait into a moment of connection. It turns irritation into a “nervous-system reset”.

And the best part? It’s completely invisible. You are walking around secretly blessing the world, planting little seeds of goodwill that no one sees but you.

Ready to play?

Next time you leave the house, leave your phone in your pocket for just 3 minutes. Look up. Find your three players.

Pause. Breathe. Be.

Have you played Metta in the Wild? You can tell us… or keep it your beautiful secret.
