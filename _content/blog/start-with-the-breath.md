"I can't meditate. My mind won't stop."

I hear this all the time. Here's the secret: nobody's mind stops. Meditation isn't about an empty head. It's about noticing where your attention went and gently bringing it back. Over and over. That's the practice.

Breathing is step one. Without the breath, we are more likely to get lost.

## What meditation is

Meditation is a way of training your attention and awareness. With practice, it helps you reach a clearer mind and a calmer, steadier emotional state.

## Ten steps to start

1. **Find a quiet space.** Somewhere you won't be interrupted. Fewer distractions, easier start.
2. **Set a timer.** Start small: 5 to 10 minutes. Add time as it gets easier.
3. **Get comfortable.** Sit on a chair, a cushion, or the floor, or lie down. Back straight, not stiff.
4. **Close your eyes.** Less to look at means more room to turn inward.
5. **Find your breath.** Feel the air move in and out. Notice your chest or belly rise and fall.
6. **When your mind wanders, come back.** It will wander. That's normal. When you notice, gently return to the breath. Noticing *is* the rep.
7. **Scan your body.** Move your attention through your body. Notice tension, warmth, or ease.
8. **Be kind to your wandering mind.** No judging. No scolding. Judgment adds tension, and tension is the opposite of what you came here for. If you feel like you're failing, just keep going.
9. **End gently.** Open your eyes slowly. Notice the room before you get up.
10. **Come back tomorrow.** Consistency beats intensity. A few minutes every day does more than an hour once a month.

## Keep it small enough to keep

A steady, tiny practice beats an ambitious one you abandon. That's the whole idea behind BE333: three minutes, three times a day, for three weeks.

Meditation is a skill. It takes time. Be patient with yourself, the same way you'd be patient with a friend learning something new.

When you're ready to explore, there are many styles to try: mindfulness, loving-kindness, body scan, and more. Find the one that fits you.

## Try this now

Set a timer for three minutes. Close your eyes. Count ten breaths. When you lose count, and you will, start again at one, with a smile.

Pause. Breathe. Be.
