You are standing in a slow line. Someone ahead of you is chewing loudly. Your shoulders are creeping toward your ears.

This is the perfect time to play a game.

I call it **Metta in the Wild**. It's gentle, a little mischievous, and completely invisible. Nobody knows you're playing. That's half the fun.

## What is metta?

Metta is an ancient Pali word often translated as *loving-kindness* or *friendliness*. It isn't romantic love. It isn't even liking everyone you meet. It's simply the wish for others to be well, happy, and free from suffering.

## The rules

The game is built on my favorite number: **3**. Play it anywhere: waiting for the train, sitting in a café, walking to work.

**Your mission:** find three players near you and secretly send each one a bloom of kindness.

### Round 1: The Neutral Player

Find someone you don't know. The barista. The person walking their dog. Someone scrolling on their phone across the room.

- **The move:** Look at them (discreetly) and silently say, *"May you be happy. May you be at ease."*
- **The goal:** Realize that just like you, they have a story, a struggle, and a wish to be happy.

### Round 2: The Challenge Player

Advanced level. Find someone who is slightly annoying you. The loud chewer. The car that cut you off. The slow walker in front of you.

- **The move:** Instead of rolling your eyes, pause. Take one deep breath to anchor yourself. Silently say, *"May you be safe. May you have peace."*
- **The goal:** Feel your own nervous system settle. You aren't doing this for them. You're doing it to release the tightness in your own chest.

### Round 3: The Mirror Player

Catch your reflection in a shop window or a mirror, or just close your eyes and picture yourself.

- **The move:** Offer the same wish inward. *"May I be kind to myself today. May I be at ease."*
- **The goal:** Remember that you deserve the same kindness you just gave away.

## Why play?

Because it shifts the world without changing a thing.

When you play, you stop scanning for threats and start scanning for humanity. A boring wait becomes a moment of connection. Irritation becomes a nervous-system reset.

And no one sees it but you. You are walking around secretly blessing the world, planting little seeds of goodwill everywhere you go.

## Try this today

Next time you leave the house, keep your phone in your pocket for three minutes. Look up. Find your three players.

Have you played? Tell me about it, or keep it your beautiful secret.

Pause. Breathe. Be.
