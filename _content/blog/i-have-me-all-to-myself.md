No matter how much you like me, you can't have me all to yourself. I don't let people have me all to themselves.

I have me all to myself.

That's solo poly, in a sentence. Here's the rest.

## What is polyamory?

Polyamory means loving more than one person. It's one branch of a bigger tree called consensual non-monogamy, which also includes open marriages, swinging, and throuples. Each has its own dynamic and structure.

Because polyamory isn't mainstream, there are no strict rules defining what it is or isn't. I love that. It means each person gets to decide whether they want to participate, and what kind of relationships actually fit them.

## What is solo poly?

Solo poly means having no desire, intention, or plan to marry or to be exclusive with anyone. It's a lot like dating: no one has authority over who you can or cannot see, because exclusivity is never on the table.

## Living it

Being solo poly is close to being single, with the freedom to do whatever you like, as long as you are open and honest where it matters.

For me, solo poly means living my life in a relationship with myself. I am committed to loving myself first and foremost, always more than I love anybody else.

By loving myself first, I never give up my freedom of self-expression, which I often express through sex. I keep the joy I get from being free: dating, meeting new people, traveling, and having all kinds of relationships, from one-night stands to lifelong connections.

## Say it upfront

Tell people early that you don't believe in, support, or participate in monogamy.

Most people, once they get to know you and want more of you, will naturally want, expect, or ask for exclusivity. Saying where you stand from the beginning sets the groundwork for what the relationship can be.

## Separate, on purpose

Solo poly also means keeping relationships separate. If you're in a relationship with me, there's no expectation that someone else gets invited in. Our relationship stays between you and me unless we both decide to add someone. Even then, there's no expectation of exclusivity or monogamy.

So if you want to date me, you have to be okay with me dating others too. Not just in the beginning. Always.

If you had me all to yourself, there wouldn't be enough of me left for me.

## Love without limits

I believe love should not be limited. I believe in loving someone so much that you don't tell them they can't love anybody else.

It might sound unconventional. For me, it's a way of life that brings immense happiness and fulfillment, and the freedom to explore travel, new people, and new passions without the constraints of traditional relationship expectations.

And solo poly doesn't mean alone. It means choosing my own happiness and well-being first, so the love and support I give the people in my life is real.
